01 — What we study

We measure who wins.

Research question: How concentrated are titles at the FIBA Asia Cup, and did the 2017 inclusion of Oceania change competitive balance? We use competitive balance theory (Rottenberg, 1956) and the Herfindahl–Hirschman Index, where 1.00 means one team wins everything.

03 — Findings

What the data shows.

i.

Cyclical dominance

Concentration peaked in 1975–2005 (HHI 0.77) when China won 14 of 16 titles.

ii.

A West Asian window

In 2007–2015 Iran won three titles and the HHI fell to 0.52.

iii.

Oceania did not spread success

Australia won all three editions since 2017 and is 18–0 overall, so HHI reached 1.00.

iv.

Uneven games too

Madarame (2017) found only 48% of Asian games were balanced, against 76% in Europe.

Home soil helps.

06 — Players

Names behind the numbers.

Method in one line.

Quantitative study of secondary data: champions of all 31 editions, grouped into four eras and measured with the HHI.
